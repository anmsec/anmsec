1Team composition and registration

  • The tournament is open to 20 teams, each composed of six players: three male and three female.
  • Each team registers under a team name of its choice and states its company or affiliation in the registration form.
  • Teams must submit the company IDs of all six members for verification before registration is approved.
  • Players participating in any match must come from the team’s approved six-member roster.

2Team name

Each team shall nominate a team name that represents its company and relates to pickleball.

An award shall be given for the best team name and logo. Once your logo is ready, send it to pmsea.pickleball@gmail.com.

3Team encounters and scoring

Each team encounter consists of three categories:

Men’s DoublesWomen’s DoublesMixed Doubles

Each category win earns one team point.

During the elimination and quarterfinal rounds, all three categories shall be played. The team winning at least two categories wins the encounter. Category-win points shall be accumulated to determine bracket standings.

4Elimination round

  • The 20 teams shall be randomly divided into four brackets of five teams each.
  • Each bracket shall follow a single round-robin format, with every team facing the other four teams in its bracket.
  • The top two teams in each bracket shall advance to the quarterfinals.

5Quarterfinal round: crossover round robin

The eight qualifying teams shall form two quarterfinal brackets:

Quarterfinal bracketQualifying teams
Bracket ETop two teams from Brackets A and B
Bracket FTop two teams from Brackets C and D
  • Each quarterfinal bracket shall follow a single round-robin format.
  • Standings shall start anew; elimination-round points shall not carry over.
  • Every team shall face the other three teams in its quarterfinal bracket.
  • The top two teams from each bracket shall advance to the semifinals.

6Semifinals: best of three

The four qualifying teams shall compete through crossover matches:

  • Semifinal 1: Bracket E Rank 1 vs. Bracket F Rank 2
  • Semifinal 2: Bracket F Rank 1 vs. Bracket E Rank 2

Each encounter shall follow this sequence:

1Men’s Doubles2Women’s Doubles3Mixed Doubles, if needed

The first team to win two games advances to the championship finals.

The two losing semifinalists shall compete for third place, using the same best-of-three format and game sequence.

7Championship finals: best of five

The championship encounter shall follow this sequence:

1Men’s Doubles2Women’s Doubles3Mixed Doubles4Mixed Doubles, new pairing, if needed5Mixed Doubles, another new pairing, if needed

The first team to win three games shall be declared champion.

For Games 4 and 5, teams must select players from their approved six-member roster. Each pair must consist of one male and one female, and no mixed-doubles partnership may be repeated within the championship encounter. Individual players may play again with a different partner.

8Game scores

Tournament stageWinning score
Elimination roundRace to 11; no deuce
QuarterfinalsRace to 11; no deuce
SemifinalsRace to 11; no deuce
Third-place playoffRace to 11; no deuce
Championship finalsRace to 15; win by two
  • For games without deuce, the next point at 10–10 wins the game.
  • For championship games, play shall continue at 14–14 until one pair leads by two points.

9Bracketing and standings

  • The initial bracket draw shall be conducted randomly and announced on tournament day.
  • Elimination and quarterfinal standings shall be based on total category-win points.
  • Procedures for resolving tied standings shall be announced by the organizers before the first match.

11Team uniforms

  • Each team shall wear its own coordinated tournament uniform.
  • A special Best-Looking Team Uniform Award shall be presented.

12Awards

Tournament winners shall receive medals and cash prizes. Prize amounts and award details shall be announced by the organizers.

13Socials Night

All participants are encouraged to attend the Socials Night on November 18. Raffle prizes may be available, subject to confirmation.

14Tournament administration

The tournament organizers reserve the right to modify the tournament format as necessary. Any changes shall be communicated to participating teams.
